## §3 The 3 findings that most block production-grade

1. **A-3 — no deadman on the watchdog (or the duty host), and the only de-correlated alert is a visible-not-fatal comment.** The longevity plane's entire safety net can vanish silently (schedule sparsity is live-proven, and the compound pinger-dead + held-chain case ends in GH's 60-day scheduled-workflow auto-disable with zero alerts). Everything else in this plane assumes someone gets told; this is the hole in "someone gets told".
2. **A-1 — the watch-the-watcher spec's 45-min window is calibrated against a cadence the live system disproves (2h–4h07m).** Duty-8, the documented next step, would ship as a permanent false-alarm lane that trains operators to ignore the X-class signal — detection quality goes *negative* exactly where the plane is supposed to be strongest.
3. **A-2 — the watchdog's 24h alert-dedup structurally breaks at >20 comments on the alert issue** (law-20 pagination), returning the 12-alerts/day spam during precisely the long incidents the latch exists for — alert fatigue on the only human-escalation channel this plane has.

(Honorable 4th: A-4 — the re-prime failure path is silent and un-latchable, the correlated-failure re-entry of the exact loop F-B killed.)
